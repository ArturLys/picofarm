PicoFarm £100

A remake of MuuMuu's Scratch farming game, rebuilt feature by feature from his devlog: plough, plant, water through the seasons, fill your silos and sell before it all rots.

Click the flag to start. Needs a keyboard and mouse.

Controls

First click
Place your farm yard. You can only farm and build inside its plot.
W A S D
Move around the map (arrow keys work too).
− Zoom +
Top right, five zoom levels.
Farm / Build
Pick a tool, then click the map. Drag windows by their title bar.
Market
Sell a whole crop at today's price.
Bottom left
Brush size, up to 4×4 tiles per click.
Bottom right
Soil fertility overlay.
A silo
Click it with no tool to see what's rotting inside.

How the year runs

1 day = 3 s · 20 days a season
Winter → Spring → Summer → Autumn
Produce rots in 20 days (40 in a cooled silo)
Land tax £1 a tile each season
Loans 5% a season
